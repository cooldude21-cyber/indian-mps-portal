Statutory Transparency & Legal Disclaimers

Citizen Public Transparency & Official Source Disclosures

This platform serves as an open, non-partisan public record registry designed to uphold democratic transparency and citizen awareness across all Members of the Indian Parliament (Lok Sabha & Rajya Sabha) and historical executive leadership from 1947 to the present day.

Statutory Legal Mandate (Section 33A, Representation of the People Act, 1951) Pursuant to Section 33A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, and landmark directives of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India (2002 & 2003 in ADR v. Union of India), every candidate seeking election to Parliament is legally obligated to submit a sworn affidavit (Form 26) disclosing their movable and immovable assets, liabilities, educational qualifications, and pending criminal cases.

About The Project

Indian Parliamentary Archive & Civic Dossier

The Indian Parliamentary Archive is an independent, non-partisan civic technology platform built to provide uncompromised visibility into the legislative history, financial disclosures, criminal filings, fund utilization, and government audit performance across Indian democracy since 1947.

🏛️ Complete Chamber Coverage Spanning all 18 Lok Sabha sessions (from 1st LS in 1952 to 18th LS in 2024), the Rajya Sabha Council of States, all Prime Ministers, and constitutional Presidents.
📊 Dual Audit & Accountability Juxtaposes official Press Information Bureau (PIB) press releases with Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) parliamentary audit reports and MoSPI MPLADS trackers.
🗺️ Tiered Territory Directory Structured state-by-state directory of all 28 States and 8 Union Territories with instant drill-down to Lok Sabha parliamentary constituencies and sitting representatives.
Real-Time Data Provenance
• Election Commission of India (ECI): Candidate Form 26 Affidavits & Gazette results.
• Digital Sansad (sansad.in): Official legislative registries of Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha secretariats.
• Association for Democratic Reforms (ADR): Civic transparency and electoral analysis.
• Comptroller & Auditor General (CAG) of India: Official performance and compliance audits tabled in Parliament.
• Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI): MPLADS portal disbursement & expenditure logs.
• Wikipedia & Wikimedia Commons: Encyclopedic historical profiles and public archive portraits.
