Statutory Transparency & Legal Disclaimers

Citizen Public Transparency & Official Source Disclosures

This platform serves as an open, non-partisan public record registry designed to uphold democratic transparency and citizen awareness across all Members of the Indian Parliament (Lok Sabha & Rajya Sabha) and historical executive leadership from 1947 to the present day.

Statutory Legal Mandate (Section 33A, Representation of the People Act, 1951) Pursuant to Section 33A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, and landmark directives of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India (2002 & 2003 in ADR v. Union of India), every candidate seeking election to Parliament is legally obligated to submit a sworn affidavit (Form 26) disclosing their movable and immovable assets, liabilities, educational qualifications, and pending criminal cases.

All financial asset declarations, educational records, criminal disclosures, and electoral term details are derived directly from verified official gazettes and statutory submissions:

3. Press Information Bureau (PIB) & National Archives of India Historic portfolios of Prime Ministers, Constituent Assembly leaders, and Presidents.
