Data Methodology

Source
First

Parliamentary facts are kept separate from live enrichment. Official Government/Parliament sources are preferred for verification; Wikipedia is optional enrichment only. The interface does not invent unavailable biographical information. Fields not supplied by the source dataset remain unavailable in the profile.

Primary Sources

Government
Records

Use these official sources to verify political identity, parliamentary membership, election results and party information.

Digital Sansad — Lok Sabha Official member list, constituency, State/UT, membership status and Lok Sabha terms. Digital Sansad — Rajya Sabha Official Rajya Sabha parliamentary information and member resources. Election Commission of India Official election results, candidate information, political-party records and statistical reports. Lok Sabha Digital Library Official parliamentary database describing member biodata, biographies and historical records. ECI — Political Parties Official registered-party and recognition information. Parliament of India — About Official explanation of Parliament, Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.
